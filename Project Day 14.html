The Fundamentals of Volleyball

Volleyball was created in 1895 in the USA. From there, it became a popular sport that is played all over the world. In volleyball, there are five skills that one can work on.

Firstly, to start all actions off in volleyball is the serve. When serving, you can choose to jump serve or to preform a jump float serve. One can also choose to do a non-jump serve.

Next comes the receive

After the receive, someone goes to set the ball

Once the ball is set, someone goes up to spike

Finally, when someone goes up to spike, someone also jumps to block them

After all of this, the process repeats. Most of the time the ralley doesn't end on a single spike, block, or serve. Most times the players will receive and use many more crazy tactics to score a point. Volleyball is an amazing sport! Players are constantly adapting and working as a team throughout the game to win and that's why it's my favorite sport.

Order of the cycle in volleyball:

  1. Serve
  2. Receive
  3. Set
  4. Spike
  5. Block
  6. Repeat
